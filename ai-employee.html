AI Employee for SMBs

A practical automation concept that helps small businesses respond to enquiries more consistently—capturing leads, qualifying intent, organizing the CRM, and prompting the sales team to follow up.

Project scope

Built around useful business actions

CAPTURE

Structured lead intake

Collect customer details and normalize them into consistent fields before routing.

PRIORITIZE

HOT / WARM / COLD routing

Use simple qualification signals to help salespeople decide which enquiry needs attention first.

RECORD

Google Sheets CRM

Keep lead information, status, summaries, and follow-up dates in a shared working sheet.

FOLLOW THROUGH

Alerts and reminders

Notify the team about priority leads and support repeatable follow-up routines.

Next step

A prototype toward an AI employee

The goal is to connect enquiry handling with real operational actions—not just answer questions in a chat window. The current scope focuses on the lead intake and follow-up workflow, with further integrations and reliability checks needed before production use.
